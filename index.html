World clock, time zone converter and satellite map

TimeZone Pro is a free online world clock. See the live local time in any city or country, compare it with your own time, and keep all your favourite places on one page that works on your phone, tablet or computer.

What you can do with TimeZone Pro

  • Add as many world clocks as you like from a database of more than 230,000 cities, towns and places.
  • See the time difference, the date and whether it is day or night in every place.
  • Convert any date and time from one time zone to another, including daylight saving changes.
  • Find your clocks on a live world map and fly to them on a satellite map.
  • Share your clocks with family and friends using one link.

Free and easy to use

Search for a city or a whole country, for example Manama, London, Tokyo or "cities in France", and press Enter to add it. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Your clocks are saved on your own device.
